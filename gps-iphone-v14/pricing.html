FAQ

Clear expectations: what is automated, what is coach work, and how supervision levels differ.

Is training automatically adjusted every day?
No. Basic is self-guided. Plus and Pro include coach reviews and adjustments. The platform provides structure and trends so a coach can make decisions reliably.
What does “game-based weekly microcycle” mean?
Training is structured around match demands and weekly rhythm (intensity, recovery, readiness). The microcycle is individualized per athlete; supervision level defines how often it is reviewed and adjusted.
What is automated right now?
Data tracking and the daily diet generator. Coaching decisions and microcycle adjustments are manual in Plus and Pro.
Can I upgrade later?
Yes. You can start with Basic and upgrade to Plus or Pro when you want more supervision.
Is this medical advice?
No. This is a training and performance system. If you have injury symptoms, consult a qualified medical professional.
